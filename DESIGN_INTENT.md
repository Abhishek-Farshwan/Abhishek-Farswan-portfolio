# Design Intent: Purposeful, Attractive, Performance-Aware

This portfolio should feel authored rather than generated. It should avoid the generic “AI interface” look without becoming sterile, flat, or overly minimal. The goal is a polished, attractive, responsive experience that feels premium and intentional while staying efficient and performant.

## Core principles

### 1. Anti-generic, not anti-beauty
- Avoid cliché “AI design” tropes: random gradient blobs, over-glossed surfaces, decorative noise, and generic soft neon overload.
- Do not chase novelty for novelty’s sake.
- The site should look sophisticated, confident, and fully intentional — not like a templated prompt output.

### 2. Attractive and responsive first
- The interface should feel premium across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Strong composition, well-judged spacing, and crisp typography matters more than gimmicks.
- The design should still feel expressive and visually rich without relying on heavy visual clutter.

### 3. Useful visual complexity, not decorative excess
- Layering, border systems, contrast, shadows, and typography should all have a purpose.
- The design can be expressive and immersive while staying disciplined.
- Keep visual richness grounded in real interface logic and content hierarchy.

### 4. Performance-aware design
- Avoid stacking multiple translucent layers, blurred backgrounds, particle effects, and expensive repeated gradients across large sections.
- Use a small set of meaningful surfaces and effects rather than expensive rendering tricks.
- The design should feel polished without draining battery, CPU, or mobile performance.

### 5. Handcrafted system language
- Keep the sci-fi terminal aesthetic, but make it refined.
- Thoughtful borders, subtle contrast, strong type, and consistent spacing create the character.
- Every element should feel chosen, not generated.

## What to avoid
- Heavy multi-layer blurred backgrounds
- Dense animated particle systems
- Excessive gradient fields and holographic overlays
- Over-stylized “AI” glassmorphism on every panel
- Visual noise that loads expensive effects without improving clarity

## What to prefer
- Strong typography and measured spacing
- Intentional contrast and material hierarchy
- Crisp panels and restrained glow or shadow use
- Clean responsive behavior on all screen sizes
- A premium, tactile look that feels designed by a person with taste

## Design question to ask before adding anything
“Does this improve the experience, clarity, or identity of the portfolio in a way that feels considered and performant?”

If the answer is no, remove it.
